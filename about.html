About Us

Paws Rescue is a registered NPO based in Pretoria CBD. Our mission is to reduce stray animal population through rescue, sterilization and education.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by Dr. Naledi Molefe, we have rescued over 1,200 animals.

Mission & Vision

  • Rescue 100 animals per year
  • 100% sterilization before adoption
  • Community education workshops
